Invite

        
Give this code to someone who should join. They paste it on the ibc page of
          their own node. The contract's rules decide whether they may join.

        

          
          
        

        
They also need the contract's source, to read what they join: send it with
          the invite.

        
      


      

        
Public

        
A public contract can be read by anyone, without signing in: its read
          functions, on your copy, run for an unknown reader (msg.sender is the zero
          address), and its code decides what that reader sees. Nobody can change anything this way.

Transactions

        
A transaction is ordered with the other members and applied by all of them.
          You approve each one with your passkey; the result appears when it has been applied.
