Your identity

On ibc, you are a key in your phone or computer: a passkey. This node never holds it. You approve with it whatever is done in your name: each transaction, and what you let apps do. Apps you connect can read for you, and your node can sign the calls you allow them, without asking each time.

Passkeys need a secure page: open this node at its https address (or at http://localhost).

Creating an identity makes a passkey on this site and registers you on this node. Signing in uses the passkey you already have here, synced to this device or on your phone. Your passkey belongs to this node's address: the node represents you on the internet.

Register on this node

Your identity is not registered here (any more). Registering authorizes this node to take part in consensus for your contracts, with your identity passkey. It cannot sign anything as you.
